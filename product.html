About this product

A 960 W DIN rail power supply for indoor industrial control panels. It converts three-phase AC input to adjustable DC output.

Key features

  • Adjustable 24–28 V DC output
  • Overload protection
  • Enclosed design for indoor installation
  • DIN rail mounting
